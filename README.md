# 🎉 Update v3.8 Is Here!

Hello everyone! Thank you for sticking with **CihuyNime**. In version **3.8** we've completely revamped your watching experience to feel far more modern, faster, and simpler. You have to update! 🚀

---

## ✨ What's New in v3.8

### 🎬 Brand-New Player
- A clean, professional player design inspired by popular streaming apps.
- Simple, neat icons that are slightly bigger and easy to tap.
- Fullscreen in just **one tap**.
- A **slimmer** progress bar with an elegant blue accent.

### ⚡ Instant Episode Switching
- Switching episodes in portrait mode now changes **instantly**, with no layered loading and no excessive animations.
- Duration and progress automatically reset to **00:00** on every episode change.

### 👆 Complete Controls
- **Double-tap** left/right to seek ±10 seconds, with an accumulated counter (e.g. +20s, -30s).
- Swipe the screen to adjust **brightness & volume**.
- Screen lock, skip intro, next episode, and thumbnail previews while scrubbing the progress bar.

### ⚙️ Everything Lives in Settings
- Every playback option now sits neatly inside the **Settings** menu (gear icon) sliding up from the bottom:
  - Video Quality
  - Playback Speed
  - Subtitle track
  - Subtitle size & color
- No more scattered settings buttons.

### 🖥️ Simple Servers
- Server choices are now simple: just **HD-1, HD-2, and HD-3**. No more long confusing lists.

### 📝 More Accurate Titles & Info
- Episode titles inside and outside the player now follow the **official episode titles**.
- Anime movie posters and banners now always show the **anime version** (never mixed up with live-action adaptations).
- Anime with no episodes yet now clearly display a **Coming Soon** message.

### 🌍 Fully English
- The entire app interface is now consistently in **English**.

---

## 📲 How to Update
Update right from inside the app via **Profile → Check for Updates**, or download the latest version from the usual official source.

Enjoy watching! 🍿
